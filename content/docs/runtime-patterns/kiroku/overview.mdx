# Kiroku Event-Store Standards

**Start with the operational invariants, then learn the append and read contract before writing service code.**

This area is the fleet’s prescriptive Kiroku guide for Keiro services. It covers the event-store rules shared by command handlers, projections, subscriptions, operations, and observability.

Keiro 0.21.0.0 requires `kiroku-store ^>=0.11.0.0` and `kiroku-store-migrations ^>=0.7.1.0`. Use the matching `shibuya-kiroku-adapter` 0.6.0.1 and `kiroku-metrics` 0.3.0.0 cohort. Apply [migrations 0012–0015 in order](/docs/runtime-patterns/migrations/operations#upgrade-the-kiroku-store-in-order): stop old writers across 0012, stop subscription workers across 0013/0014, and plan the 0015 index-build write pause. Store 0.10 adds validated group/capacity types, target-bound checkpoints, typed decode refusals and transactional resize; Store 0.11 adds [bounded inspection](/docs/runtime-patterns/kiroku/inspection) and the opt-in originated stream head. Extend exhaustive effect/error matches and rebuild direct record construction. Store 0.8 adds the retryable `TransientTransactionFailure` constructor; extend exhaustive error matches and apply [bounded retry](/docs/runtime-patterns/kiroku/transactions-and-projections). Earlier releases also extended the `Store` effect, so update custom or mock interpreters when crossing them: 0.5 makes the [first-run checkpoint decision explicit](/docs/runtime-patterns/kiroku/subscriptions) and adds the transactional reset; 0.6 adds the [visible global head](/docs/runtime-patterns/kiroku/append-and-read); 0.7 adds [replay-history retention leases](/docs/runtime-patterns/kiroku/history-retention), the transaction-scoped stream guard, and the `HistoryRetentionActive` refusal. On the migration side, `0009` publishes the frozen [checkpoint relation](/docs/runtime-patterns/kiroku/checkpoint-inventory) and corrected `0010` plus `0011` install and converge the retention schema on `kiroku.uuidv7()`. Databases that applied withdrawn `0010` require the [guarded checksum recovery](/docs/runtime-patterns/migrations/operations) before normal migration; Hackage deprecates migration releases 0.3.2.0 and 0.3.2.1.
