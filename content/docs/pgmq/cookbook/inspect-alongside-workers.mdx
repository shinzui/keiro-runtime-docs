---
title: "Inspect queues beside workers"
description: "Combine worker capacity and non-destructive inspection without sharing leases."
---

Run workers with their normal consume/acknowledgement pool. Give `pgmq-inspect` its own login, finite-budget connections and shared metrics feed. Inspection pages use SELECT and do not reserve messages; consumption continues to own visibility leases and retries.

Start at the default polling cadence and compare worker latency/throughput with inspection enabled. Shared database CPU/IO remains a source of interference. Prefer queue-scoped views and shared subscriptions over per-tab all-queue polling. On socket overflow, re-read inspection HTTP; do not call the worker's consuming read to repair a display.

For partitioned queues, retain polling even after migration 0007: parent insert notifications are unsupported. A `push:true` snapshot does not prove a healthy listener or installed trigger. Before exposing a browser, preserve exact int64 IDs in its decoder and verify database privileges deny consumption with the inspection login.

See [deployment](/docs/pgmq/how-to/deploy-inspection), [feed recovery](/docs/pgmq/how-to/recover-inspection-feeds), and the [Shibuya adapter](/docs/integrations/shibuya-pgmq-adapter).
