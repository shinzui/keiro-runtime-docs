---
title: "Inspection observes queues without consuming"
description: "Ownership, freshness and resource costs of inspection."
---

Consumption reads acquire visibility leases and advance read counts. Inspection uses non-destructive reads, leaving `vt`, `read_ct` and `last_read_at` unchanged. A message remains available to workers after inspection.

Pages are moving views. Concurrent producer commits and archival can insert a lower ID behind a cursor; restart from the first page to refresh. Metrics notifications are wake-up hints. Polling converges even when a listener disconnects or a partitioned queue cannot notify.

Dedicated pools isolate connection capacity, while PostgreSQL CPU and IO remain shared. Frequent all-queue metrics still scan queues. Upstream workload evidence observed interference and does not establish a universally safe polling interval. Measure worker SLOs with inspection enabled before choosing cadence.

See the [server reference](/docs/pgmq/reference/inspection-server) and [deployment procedure](/docs/pgmq/how-to/deploy-inspection).
