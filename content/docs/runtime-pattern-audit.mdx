---
title: Read a runtime pattern audit
description: Match a canonical rule and its revision to concrete work evidence.
---

The [runtime pattern catalog](/docs/runtime-patterns) shows rules available to agents. A catalog page does not prove that an agent used the rule or met it.

## Identify the rule and revision

A work record must name the canonical resource URI and the full catalog commit. Identify the applicable criterion when the owner supplies one. Most concepts have no assessment criteria. Do not invent an assessment ID for them.

The [behavior conformance standard](/docs/runtime-patterns/keiro/behavior-conformance) supplies `PAT-1` and five criteria. Use its published applicability to decide whether the standard covers the service.

## Inspect the evidence

For `conformance-report-generated`, inspect the generated CI report for each declared aggregate. Check that it covers the nine key sets.

For `conformance-gate-passes`, inspect the gate result and report. Check for pending, missing, duplicate, stale, and failed keys.

For `unverified-keys-kept-honest`, inspect the review of evidence strength. Passed witnesses can still be unverified.

For `gaps-closed-by-witnesses`, inspect executed witnesses or the reconciled specification change. A deleted obligation does not close a gap.

For the advisory `fail-on-unverified-deliberate` criterion, inspect the policy decision and guard coverage. The source does not require that policy for every aggregate.

## Record a reviewable claim

Use a record with these fields:

```text
Resource: mori://shinzui/keiro-runtime-patterns/docs/keiro-behavior-conformance
Catalog commit: <full SHA displayed on the published page>
Criterion: conformance-gate-passes
Evidence: <CI run, aggregate report, and gate result>
Assessment: <met, unmet, or insufficient evidence, with a reason>
```

A statement that an agent consulted a rule is evidence of consultation. It does not establish implementation conformance. Review the cited output and its scope before accepting the claim.

## Read publication evidence

The catalog preserves material timestamps, source actors, supplied reviews, and declared library watermarks. A supplied review matches the material revision only when its document timestamp matches. Older reviews remain history.

Watermarks record the owner's source review boundary. They do not prove the dependencies are current. Historical examples and candidate features retain source qualification. Supplementary diagrams explain the quoted rules and carry separate content bindings.
